Key Features

01 🗂️ 3-Tier Folder Structure

  • ▹ Languages → Sources → Folders: Organize words with granular nesting, such as German → Grammar & Verbs → Modal Verbs.
  • ▹ Independent language pairs per folder (e.g. German → English, Spanish → English, Japanese → French).
  • ▹ Cascading filters to narrow your list by Language, Source, or Folder in a tap.

02 🎨 German Article & Gender Colors

  • ▹ Instant recognition with signature article color coding: der, die, and das.
  • ▹ Distinct badges for verbs, adjectives, adverbs, and phrases.
  • ▹ One-tap filters for der, die, das, and favorites.

03 🧠 Active Recall Quizzes

  • ▹ Multiple Choice: 4 dynamic options with instant emerald/rose feedback.
  • ▹ 3D Flashcards: flip to reveal and self-rate "Need Practice" or "Got It!".
  • ▹ Spelling test with accent-tolerant checking, plus a Retry Mistakes mode.

04 📅 Calendar & Streaks

  • ▹ Month grid with glowing indicators showing how many words you added each day.
  • ▹ Daily inspection: tap any day to review words, listen, or quick-add.
  • ▹ Hero streak tracker for your active daily learning streak.

05 🔊 Native Text-To-Speech

  • ▹ Authentic pronunciation via native TTS with automatic audio-focus ducking.
  • ▹ 29+ languages from German and English to Japanese, Arabic, Bengali, and Hebrew.
  • ▹ Configurable: fine-tune speech rate and pitch, with auto-pronounce on save or in quiz.

06 🔒 Offline-First & Cloud Sync

  • ▹ Local Room database for instant, fully offline reads and writes.
  • ▹ Google Sign-In via Credential Manager, or explore instantly in Guest / Offline mode.
  • ▹ Two-way Firestore sync plus complete JSON backup export and import.
